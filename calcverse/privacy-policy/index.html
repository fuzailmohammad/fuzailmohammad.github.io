2. Core Privacy Principles

CalcVerse is engineered around strict on-device computation and privacy-first architectural standards:

  • On-Device Calculation Architecture: All mathematical calculations, unit conversions, financial projections, and health metric calculations are executed 100% locally on your device hardware.
  • Zero Transmission of Calculation Data: Numeric entries, formulas, intermediate results, BMI values, loan amounts, and tax figures never leave your device and are never transmitted to FuzailVerse or remote servers.
  • No User Account Required: CalcVerse functions completely without user registration, sign-up forms, email credentials, social logins, or profile creation.
  • No Data Sale or Commercial Brokering: We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade any user information, device identifiers, or usage logs with third parties or data brokers.
  • Transparent Diagnostic Telemetry: The application utilizes standard Google Firebase libraries solely to monitor technical stability, app crashes, and aggregate performance metrics, with advertising identifier collection explicitly disabled.

3. Standard and Scientific Calculator

The Standard and Scientific Calculator modules process everyday arithmetic and advanced mathematical operations (including trigonometry, logarithms, powers, factorials, and constants).

Input & Evaluation Handling: When you type digits, operators, or scientific functions, the mathematical expression is evaluated locally in volatile system memory (RAM) using a custom mathematical evaluation engine with high-precision decimal representation (BigDecimal).

Real-Time State: Intermediate calculations and preview results are generated dynamically in memory as you type and are not written to disk until evaluated.

Local History Storage: Upon tapping the equal button ("="), the evaluated expression and its result are recorded locally on your device to enable the calculation history feature.

Stored History Attributes: Stored history records contain only the expression string, result string, category ("Standard" or "Scientific"), timestamp, and favorite flag. Calculation expressions and results are never transmitted off your device.

4. Unit Converter

The Unit Converter allows you to convert measurement values across 12 distinct metric and imperial categories: Length, Area, Volume, Weight (Mass), Temperature, Speed, Time, Data Storage, Pressure, Energy, Power, and Angle.

Local Formulaic Conversion: All unit conversions are computed purely on-device using fixed mathematical conversion factors and exact decimal formulas. Conversion inputs and results remain strictly in volatile memory.

No External APIs or Network Requests: The Unit Converter operates completely offline and makes zero API calls or network connections to remote servers.

Currency Conversion Clarification: Live currency conversion and exchange-rate synchronization are temporarily disabled in this release. The application does not fetch currency market feeds or connect to financial rate APIs.

5. BMI Calculator

The BMI Calculator enables you to calculate Body Mass Index (BMI) by entering your height (centimeters), weight (kilograms), age, and gender.

Volatile In-Memory Processing: Your biometric inputs (height, weight, age, gender) and calculated BMI values exist solely within temporary UI memory (RAM) during your active session. They are immediately recomputed or cleared when you modify values or leave the screen.

No Disk Persistence: BMI inputs, categories, and healthy weight ranges are never saved to the local Room database, DataStore preferences, or device storage.

Zero External Transmission: None of the entered biometrics or results are transmitted to any server or third party.

Informational Medical Disclaimer: The BMI Calculator is provided strictly for educational and general fitness reference. It does not constitute medical advice, health diagnosis, or treatment recommendations, and does not serve as a clinical medical device.

6. Age Calculator

The Age Calculator computes your exact chronological age in years, months, and days, along with a detailed total duration breakdown (total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes, seconds), next birthday countdown, day of the week born, and astrological zodiac sign.

Processing Model: Date of birth (DOB) and reference target dates are selected locally and calculated entirely in volatile memory using native Android calendar APIs.

No Local Storage: Your date of birth is processed ephemerally and is not saved to any database, file, or preferences cache on your device.

Zero External Transmission: Dates entered in the Age Calculator are never sent to external servers or logged in diagnostic telemetry.

7. GST Calculator

The GST Calculator computes Goods and Services Tax for financial transactions based on the entered base amount, tax rate percentage (standard 3%, 5%, 12%, 18%, 28% slabs, or custom rates), tax mode (GST Exclusive / GST Inclusive), and interstate transaction designation.

On-Device Calculation: Tax calculations derive the net amount, GST amount, gross total, and tax breakdown splits (CGST + SGST or IGST) entirely within local device memory.

No Financial Transactions: CalcVerse is purely an arithmetic calculator. It does not link to bank accounts, process payment cards, initiate financial transfers, or store invoices.

No Data Retention: Entered transaction amounts and tax calculations are processed ephemerally in RAM and are not written to device storage or transmitted to any external server.

8. EMI Calculator

The EMI Calculator allows you to project monthly loan installments based on principal amount, annual interest rate, and loan tenure (in years or months), generating an estimated monthly amortization schedule.

Local Mathematical Processing: All EMI formulas, principal-versus-interest ratios, and monthly amortization installment schedules are generated locally on-device using standard financial math formulas.

No Persistence or Transmission: Loan amounts, interest figures, and schedule records exist only in transient memory during screen usage and are not stored in local databases or transmitted externally.

Financial Disclaimer: EMI calculations and amortization schedules are estimates intended solely for personal budgeting and illustrative purposes. They do not constitute a loan offer, pre-approval, credit guarantee, or professional financial advice.

9. Local Storage and Calculation History

CalcVerse provides an on-device calculation history feature powered by an embedded SQLite database managed via Android Jetpack Room (database name: "calcverse_db", table: "calculation_history").

What Is Stored: Only completed Standard and Scientific calculations evaluated using the equal button ("=") are stored. Each record includes the mathematical expression, computed result, category, timestamp, and a favorite flag.

User History Management: You maintain total control over your calculation history directly within the app:

  • View & Search: Filter history entries by category (All, Standard, Scientific, etc.) or search specific expressions and results using the built-in search bar.
  • Favorites: Star calculations to keep them bookmarked in a dedicated Favorites view.
  • Individual Deletion: Delete any individual calculation record immediately by tapping its delete icon.
  • Clear All History: Purge the entire calculation history database in a single action.
  • Toggle History Display: In Settings, you can disable the "Show History" preference at any time.
  • Android Cloud Backup Configuration: In AndroidManifest.xml, android:allowBackup is explicitly configured as "false" alongside custom empty and XML configurations. This setup instructs the standard Android OS to exclude the app's local Room database and DataStore preferences from Google Drive cloud backups and system ADB backups. Note that while standard cloud backup is disabled, physical device-to-device migration or proprietary OEM hardware transfer tools (such as manufacturer device-clone utilities) operate outside cloud backup rules and may transfer on-device sandbox files during direct physical device clones. Users wishing to prevent transfer during device migration should purge their history and clear application storage prior to transferring devices.

To provide a personalized user experience, CalcVerse stores configuration preferences locally using Android Jetpack DataStore Preferences ("calcverse_preferences.preferences_pb") in your device’s private app sandbox.

Verified Locally Persisted Preferences in Current Release:

  • Accent Color: Chosen interface accent color (Neon Green, Cyan, Amber, Purple, Rose, etc.).

  • Button Sound: Audible click sounds on key presses (default: disabled).
  • Auto Calculate: Live expression evaluation during typing (default: enabled).
  • Show History: Display history entry points and records (default: enabled).
  • Remember Last Calculator: Re-open the last active calculator upon app launch (default: disabled).
  • Favorite Calculators: Shortcut preferences for pinned calculator tools.
  • Recent Searches: The top 10 search queries entered in the home screen search bar (can be cleared in-app at any time).

11. Firebase Analytics and Usage Telemetry

CalcVerse integrates Google Firebase Analytics (com.google.firebase:firebase-analytics:23.2.0) to evaluate application adoption, feature engagement, and overall software stability in aggregate.

Automatically Collected Telemetry: Firebase Analytics automatically records technical metrics, including app open events, session durations, device model, operating system version, app version, language preference, and coarse geographic region derived by Google from the device's network IP address.

Source Code Audit: The CalcVerse Kotlin source code does not log any custom calculation expressions, mathematical results, financial values, biometric metrics, or personal identifiers to Firebase Analytics.

Advertising ID Collection Disabled: In AndroidManifest.xml, the application explicitly declares: . This configuration turns off Google Advertising ID (AAID) collection by the Firebase Analytics SDK, preventing analytics telemetry from being linked to a personalized advertising profile.

Pseudonymous Identifiers & Attribution Notice: While Google Advertising ID collection is disabled, Firebase Analytics automatically assigns a randomized, pseudonymous Firebase Installation ID (app-instance identifier) to maintain session continuity and aggregate reporting across app launches. We do not claim that telemetry is completely anonymous or untracked; rather, it is pseudonymized and disassociated from personal identity. Furthermore, store install attribution mechanisms (such as the Google Play Install Referrer) and Android Privacy Sandbox attribution APIs remain integrated at the platform library level to measure Google Play install channels.

Data Handling & Processing: Telemetry is transmitted securely via HTTPS/TLS to Google infrastructure and is processed in accordance with Google’s Privacy Policy. FuzailVerse receives only aggregate statistical reports and does not receive or maintain individual user identifiers.

12. Firebase Crashlytics and Performance Monitoring

To ensure software reliability and high performance across diverse Android devices, CalcVerse incorporates Firebase Crashlytics (com.google.firebase:firebase-crashlytics:20.0.6) and Firebase Performance Monitoring (com.google.firebase:firebase-perf:22.0.5).

Crash Diagnostics: In the event of an unexpected crash or application freeze, Crashlytics automatically collects technical diagnostic data, including stack traces, exception messages, device manufacturer and model, operating system version, device memory/storage status, and application build version at the moment of failure. Source code inspection confirms that CalcVerse configures no custom user IDs, email tags, or calculation parameters in Crashlytics reports.

Automated Performance Monitoring Instrumentation: The Firebase Performance Monitoring Gradle plugin and SDK automatically instrument the application at compile time. This telemetry includes:

  • App Startup Traces: Measuring the duration from process launch until initial application frames render.
  • Screen Rendering Traces: Tracking slow-rendering and frozen frames across Jetpack Compose UI transitions.
  • App Lifecycle Traces: Measuring the duration of time the application spends active in the foreground versus background.
  • Automatic Network Request Monitoring: The Performance SDK automatically instruments standard network client libraries (such as OkHttp and HttpURLConnection) to record the latency, payload size, response code, and destination domain of outbound network requests initiated by active SDKs (such as Firebase telemetry uploads). CalcVerse's own application code contains no custom network clients, but underlying SDK communications are automatically measured for latency and failure rates.

15. Categories of Data Not Collected

To ensure complete clarity regarding your privacy, CalcVerse distinguishes clearly between data processed locally, data saved on-device, and data transmitted externally:

  • Personal Identification: We do not collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, home addresses, or physical identity details.
  • Geographic Location (GPS vs. IP-Derived): CalcVerse declares zero Android location permissions (ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION, ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION, ACCESS_BACKGROUND_LOCATION) and never accesses device GPS, Wi-Fi location sensors, or cellular towers. When third-party SDKs (such as Firebase Analytics) communicate over the network, Google servers receive the device's public IP address, from which Google estimates coarse, regional-level geographical metrics (e.g., country or state/city level). This IP-derived estimation occurs on Google's servers and does not constitute device-level precision location access by CalcVerse.
  • Camera and Microphone: We do not declare, request, or use camera or microphone permissions.
  • Photos, Media, and Storage: We do not request broad storage permissions (READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE, or READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) and cannot access your photos, videos, or documents.
  • Health & Medical Records: BMI calculations (height, weight, age, gender) are computed in volatile RAM only; we do not collect, store, or share health records.
  • Financial & Payment Credentials: GST and EMI calculations are computed in volatile RAM only; we do not collect or process bank account numbers, credit cards, or financial transaction logs.
  • User Accounts & Authentication: We do not maintain user databases, passwords, or authentication profiles.
  • Advertising Identifiers: We explicitly disable Google Advertising ID collection via manifest metadata.

16. Data Retention and Deletion

Local Calculation History: Stored in the on-device SQLite database until you delete specific calculations, clear all history via the in-app history controls, or uninstall CalcVerse from your device.

Local Preferences: Retained in the on-device DataStore file until you reset app preferences or uninstall the application.

Android Cloud Backup: Because android:allowBackup="false" is configured, local calculation history and preferences are excluded from Google Drive backups.

Third-Party Telemetry Retention: Data collected by Google Firebase SDKs is governed by Google’s lifecycle retention policies. Crashlytics logs are typically retained by Google for up to 90 days before automated deletion. Firebase Analytics event data is retained according to Google's standard rolling retention schedules (configured for standard aggregation periods, up to 14 months). We do not promise instantaneous or permanent deletion after fixed intervals beyond Google's published lifecycle policies. Because telemetry is pseudonymous and not linked to individual user accounts or email addresses, FuzailVerse possesses no technical mechanism to search, identify, or delete individual telemetry records upon request.

Complete Local Deletion: You can completely purge all local CalcVerse data at any time through Android System Settings: Settings → Apps → CalcVerse → Storage & Cache → Clear Storage.

17. Security and Data Protection

We implement rigorous defense-in-depth technical safeguards to protect your data:

  • Android Application Sandbox: Local database files and preferences are stored exclusively within the private internal application directory (/data/data/com.fuzailverse.calcverse/), isolated by Linux UID permissions so other apps cannot inspect them.
  • Cleartext Traffic Policy: In AndroidManifest.xml, android:usesCleartextTraffic is explicitly configured as "false". This instructs the Android operating system's network security config to reject unencrypted HTTP traffic from standard system APIs and compliant networking libraries (such as OkHttp). While this attribute configures platform network components, it does not independently dictate low-level socket behaviors of non-compliant native code. CalcVerse integrates no custom HTTP clients or proprietary native network libraries; all outbound connections are strictly performed by official Google Firebase SDKs using modern encrypted TLS/HTTPS.
  • Zero Custom Backend Exposure: CalcVerse does not maintain custom cloud servers, REST APIs, or cloud databases. There is no central server infrastructure containing your calculations that could be compromised.
  • Realistic Security Disclaimer: While we enforce modern Android security best practices, no software or operating system can guarantee absolute invulnerability against device-level rooting, hardware compromise, or OS-level tampering.
